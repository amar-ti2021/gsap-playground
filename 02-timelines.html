GSAP Timelines

First in the timeline sequence. Uses tl.to() to move the box 400px to the right. It inherits its duration from the timeline's defaults.

Second in the sequence. Uses tl.from() to animate the box FROM an initial state (off-screen left, invisible, small) to its final position.

Third and final animation. Uses tl.to() to rotate the box 360 degrees. This tween automatically starts after the previous one is complete.
